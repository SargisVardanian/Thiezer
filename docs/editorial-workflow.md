# Thiezer Armenia Editorial Workflow

## Goal

Publish one reliable daily Armenia briefing that can be adapted into three languages and four surfaces.

## Daily loop

1. Collect story candidates and source links.
2. Choose the lead story and two to four supporting items.
3. Write the Armenian base version first.
4. Translate and adapt into Russian and English.
5. Publish full version on site.
6. Cut platform-specific variants for Telegram, Facebook, and Instagram.
7. Track what performed best and update tomorrow's angle.

## Suggested source buckets

- Government and ministry updates.
- Business and economy developments.
- Civil society and public-service changes.
- Culture, tourism, and diaspora activity.

## File workflow

- Daily drafts live in `content/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD.json`.
- The JSON structure holds headline, summary, and social caption for each language.
- Once you define the final CMS or backend, these drafts can be moved or imported without changing the editorial fields.
